Workflow best practices

  • Use turbo for fast English transcripts and medium or large for multilingual or higher-stakes accuracy.
  • Keep audio as mono 16 kHz or 32 kHz WAV when possible to reduce decode issues and make results more consistent.
  • Split long recordings by speaker or meeting section so retries are cheap and QA is easier.
  • Export both TXT and SRT/VTT so you have a readable transcript and time-aligned captions.
  • Review named entities, acronyms, and numbers manually, especially in consulting, legal, or medical material.
  • For privacy-sensitive work, keep uploads and outputs in a dedicated local folder with retention rules.
